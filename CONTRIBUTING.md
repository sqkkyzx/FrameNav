# Contributing to FrameNav

Thank you for contributing to FrameNav.

Unless you explicitly state otherwise, any contribution intentionally submitted
for inclusion in FrameNav is provided under the Apache License, Version 2.0,
consistent with section 5 of that license. You must have the right to submit the
contribution under those terms.

Please:

- describe the hardware and camera model used for device-facing changes;
- include reproducible test steps where practical;
- keep credentials, pairing material, private photographs, and device-specific
  secrets out of commits;
- identify any third-party code and preserve its copyright and license notices;
- do not submit confidential Sony documentation, leaked firmware, encryption
  keys, or other material that cannot lawfully be redistributed.

For files inherited from AlphaLoc, retain the existing attribution and the
`FrameNav modification of AlphaLoc` notice. New source files should include:

```text
Copyright 2026 FrameNav contributors
SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
```

FrameNav is independent of Sony and AlphaLoc's original author. Avoid wording
that implies official endorsement by either party.
